# Very Hard artwork

Three original square images generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized to 1024 × 1024 WebP for this game. No external image service is used during play.

## A plate of baked beans

Asset: `vh-beans.webp`

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square image for an extremely difficult picture-card jigsaw. Overhead macro food photograph of a large plain white plate filled edge-to-edge with hundreds of small British baked beans in glossy orange-red tomato sauce. The plate fills the frame, its curved white rim only barely visible at the extreme corners. Dense natural irregular arrangement of similar bean shapes with tiny unique highlights and subtle sauce patterns, all sharply focused from corner to corner. Real appetising food photography, soft diffuse daylight, uniform scale, no cutlery, garnish, toast, text or watermark. One continuous square composition, not a collage.
